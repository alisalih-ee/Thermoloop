# ThermoLoop Requirements (draft v0.1)

## Goal
Hold a small aluminum block at a chosen temperature and prove it with automated tests.

## Targets
- Set-point range: ambient to 80 °C
- Steady-state error: within ±0.5 °C
- Settling time: under [decide after the first test] seconds
- Overshoot: under [decide after the first test] %
- Telemetry: one reading over UART every 100 ms

## Safety
- Heater shuts off above 100 °C (second thermocouple as independent check)
- Heater shuts off if a thermocouple is disconnected
- Inline thermal fuse and a fuse on the 12 V input

## How each target gets tested
- Accuracy: ice bath (0 °C) and boiling water (about 100 °C)
- Step response and stability: automated Python tests
- Fault response: unplug a thermocouple and measure how fast the heater turns off
